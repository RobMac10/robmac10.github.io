About me

I am an independent writer, historian and educator. I specialise in Australian pioneer family history, colonial and Aboriginal history told from more than one point of view, and interactive resources for young readers.

I research and write with primary sources: ships’ logs, birth, death and marriage records, police gazettes, parish registers and directories. My work aims to be balanced and factual. It respects the resilience of settlers and pioneers, and it also respects Aboriginal sovereignty and resistance.

I also make classroom materials, including quizzes, bibliographies, primary-source “cold case” investigations and teacher packs. From 2017 to 2018 I was a contracted STEM resource writer for R.I.C. Publications.

My books are published under the author name PW RJ McKean. I am based in the Perth and Mandurah region of Western Australia.
